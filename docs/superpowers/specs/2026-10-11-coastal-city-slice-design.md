# Coastal city playable slice

Approved direction: translate the title illustration into a layered coastal royal city, beginning with one walkable route. Reuse the five supplied assets and the existing first-region world.

This iteration adds a west-side ascent from the existing street, a six-metre residential terrace, a second ascent to a twelve-metre belvedere, and an arcaded overlook. A distant palace, tiered residential skyline and coastal viaduct establish the composition. Existing story anchors, expeditions and south meadow connection remain reachable. Distant architecture is scenery, not an enterable district.

One domain surface layout defines render and collision heights. Ramp surfaces are visibly sloped paving with flanking stepped masonry. Parapets bound the raised route. Camera collision uses the same architectural boundaries. New static geometry shares materials and is merged by spatial district; distant scenery has no physics or dynamic shadow casters. Existing imported geometry is reused at explicitly authored base heights.

Acceptance: walk from the street through both ascents to the overlook and back; no holes or sealed entrances; correct foot placement and safe camera near parapets; clear palace silhouette from street and vista. Verify domain/geometry regressions, typecheck, production build and browser screenshots. Android performance is a measurement target, not a claim before device testing. This is a city-layout prototype, not final commissioned architecture.
